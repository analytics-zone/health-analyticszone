The Science of Your Health

We don't just display data. We interpret your biometrics based on clinical research and peer-reviewed studies.

Cardiorespiratory Fitness (VO₂ Max)

Often considered the single strongest predictor of lifespan. Our analytics evaluate your estimated VO₂ Max against population norms to assess your physiological age and long-term mortality risk.

Why it matters: A landmark study of 122,007 patients found that "elite" cardiorespiratory fitness was associated with an 80% reduction in mortality risk compared to low fitness.

Source: Mandsager K, et al. (JAMA Network Open, 2018).

Autonomic Balance (HRV)

We analyze Heart Rate Variability (specifically rMSSD) to measure the balance between your sympathetic (fight-or-flight) and parasympathetic (rest-and-digest) nervous systems.

The Science: Higher HRV is generally linked to better cardiovascular health and stress resilience. It serves as a sensitive marker for systemic inflammation and recovery status.

Source: Task Force of the European Society of Cardiology (1996); Shaffer & Ginsberg (2017).

Functional Mobility (Gait Speed)

Walking speed is often called "the sixth vital sign" in clinical settings. We track your gait speed trends to detect early signs of functional decline or neurological changes.

Clinical Relevance: Analysis of 34,485 older adults demonstrated that gait speed is directly associated with 5-year and 10-year survival rates across all age groups.

Source: Studenski S, et al. (JAMA, 2011).

Sleep & Glymphatic Clearance

We don't just track hours slept; we analyze Deep Sleep (NREM Stage 3) duration. This is critical for the "glymphatic system," the brain's waste clearance mechanism.

Mechanism: During deep sleep, the glymphatic system becomes active, clearing neurotoxic waste products like beta-amyloid that accumulate during wakefulness.

Source: Xie L, et al. (Science, 2013); Reddy & van der Werf (2020).
