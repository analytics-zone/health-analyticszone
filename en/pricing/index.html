Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a free version?

The 7-day free trial gives you full access to evaluate the app. After the trial, a subscription is required to continue using all features.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel your subscription at any time through the App Store. Your access continues until the end of your current billing period.

Do you offer refunds?

Refunds are handled through Apple according to their App Store policies.

Is my data safe if I cancel?

Your health data remains in Apple Health regardless of your subscription status. Health Analytics only reads your data—it doesn't store it separately.

Do you offer family or team plans?

Not currently. Each subscription is for individual use.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. The prices shown are the complete prices. No additional fees, no in-app purchases for extra features.
