Privacy Policy for Health Analytics

Last updated: January 2026

Introduction

We believe your health data belongs to you. This Privacy Policy describes how Health Analytics ("we", "our", or "us") handles your data.

In short: We do not collect, store, or share your personal health data on our servers. Everything happens locally on your device.

HealthKit Data Integration

Our application integrates with Apple's HealthKit to provide you with analytics and insights. Here is how we handle this data:

  • Collection: We request permission to read specific health metrics from HealthKit (such as workouts, heart rate, etc.) directly on your device.
  • Processing: All data processing and analysis are performed locally on your iPhone or Apple Watch. Your raw health data is never sent to our servers.
  • Exporting: The app allows you to export your processed health data for your own use (e.g. CSV). This export is initiated by you and stays under your control.

Data Storage and Security

Since we do not transmit your health data to any external server, your data remains as secure as your device. We do not maintain a database of user health information.

Third-Party Sharing

We do not share, sell, or disclose your HealthKit data to third parties, advertising networks, or data brokers.

Your Rights

You have full control over your data. You can manage HealthKit permissions at any time via the "Health" app on your iPhone (Sources tab). You can also choose to delete the app, which will remove all local data associated with it.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@analyticszone.com.
