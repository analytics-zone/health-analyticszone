About Health Analytics

Health data should be useful, portable, and owned by the user.

Our Mission

Health Analytics exists to make Apple Health data:

📖 Readable

Clear dashboards and summaries that make sense

🤝 Shareable

Doctor-ready PDF reports for medical consultations

📤 Exportable

CSV/JSON/Markdown for analysis and integration

✅ Trustworthy

Transparent methods with scientific references

🔒 Private

100% local processing—your data never leaves your device

🎯 Actionable

Insights you can use to improve your health and longevity

Core Principles

🔬

Science First

We explain what each metric means, why it matters, and what the limitations are. Every health metric explanation links to authoritative references. No black-box "health scores" without explanation.

🔒

Privacy by Design

  • Local processing only
  • No accounts required
  • No tracking or analytics SDKs
  • No data transmitted to servers
  • Your data stays on your device
👁️

Transparency

  • Clear formulas and interpretations
  • Measured vs estimated labels
  • Confounders highlighted
  • Open about capabilities and limits
🔗

Interoperability

  • CSV for spreadsheets
  • JSON for developers
  • PDF for healthcare providers
  • Markdown for documentation

What We Are

Health Analytics is a companion app to Apple Health. We help you understand, visualize, and export your HealthKit data.

  • A data visualization tool
  • An export utility
  • A scientific context provider
  • A privacy-respecting app

What We Are NOT

Health Analytics is not a medical device or replacement for healthcare providers.

  • Not a diagnostic tool
  • Not an emergency response system
  • Not a treatment recommendation engine
  • For informational purposes only

Editorial Standards

Our health content follows strict standards

📚 Authoritative Sources

Every metric explanation cites WHO, CDC, ADA, AHA, or peer-reviewed journals.

📍 Data Provenance

We clearly label whether values are measured directly or estimated.

⚠️ Limitations Disclosed

We explain confounders and accuracy limits for every metric.

🔄 Regular Review

Content is periodically updated for accuracy. Last review: January 2026.
