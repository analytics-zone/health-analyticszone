Who It's For

Health Analytics serves anyone who wants to understand, share, and own their health data. Here's how different users benefit.

Patients

Prepare and share organized summaries for appointments

Managing a health condition means tracking symptoms, vitals, and treatments over time. Health Analytics helps you:

  • Prepare for appointments - Generate clear PDF reports showing your trends
  • Track multiple conditions - Monitor various metrics in one unified view
  • Share selectively - Export only what's relevant for each consultation
  • Keep records - Maintain your own copies of health data

Example use cases: - Pre-appointment summary for your cardiologist showing heart rate trends and blood pressure - Diabetes management reports with glucose readings and activity levels - Sleep quality documentation for fatigue-related consultations

Athletes & Coaches

Track training trends and export to analysis tools

Serious training requires serious data. Health Analytics gives you:

  • Training load visibility - See workouts, heart rate zones, and recovery metrics
  • Trend analysis - Track VO₂ max, resting heart rate, and HRV over time
  • Export flexibility - CSV and JSON exports integrate with training platforms
  • Recovery insights - Sleep and HRV data help optimize rest periods

Example use cases: - Weekly training summaries with workout volume and intensity - Recovery tracking using HRV and sleep quality trends - Cardio fitness progression monitoring via VO₂ max estimates

Families

Help manage health information for loved ones

Whether caring for aging parents or managing children's health, having organized data helps:

  • Coordinate care - Share relevant health summaries with family members
  • Prepare for appointments - Help loved ones arrive with organized information
  • Track changes - Notice trends that might warrant attention
  • Keep records - Maintain family health documentation

Example use cases: - Activity and sleep summaries for an elderly parent - Growth and activity tracking for children - Medication and vital sign logs for family health records

Developers & Researchers

Structured exports for analysis and integration

Building tools or conducting research with health data? Health Analytics provides:

  • Clean data exports - Well-structured CSV and JSON formats
  • Comprehensive coverage - Access to all HealthKit data types
  • Flexible time ranges - Export specific periods for analysis
  • Consistent formatting - Reliable data structure for programmatic use

Example use cases: - Personal health data analysis projects - Research studies requiring participant data exports - Integration with custom health tracking systems - Building personal health dashboards

Privacy-Conscious Users

Anyone who values data ownership

If you care about where your health data goes, Health Analytics is built for you:

  • No cloud required - Everything processes locally
  • No accounts - Start using immediately without signing up
  • No tracking - Zero analytics or advertising SDKs
  • Full control - Export your data anytime, in formats you choose

Is Health Analytics Right for You?

Health Analytics is ideal if you:

  • Use an iPhone and optionally an Apple Watch
  • Want to understand your health data beyond Apple Health's default views
  • Need to share health information with doctors or family
  • Prefer local, privacy-respecting apps
  • Value scientific context for health metrics

Not the best fit if you: - Need real-time monitoring or medical alerts - Require integration with specific healthcare systems - Want social features or community comparisons - Need Android support
