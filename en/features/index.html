Features

Features

Health Analytics transforms your Apple Health data into actionable insights with powerful visualization, scientific context, and flexible export options.

Unified Dashboard

See your most important metrics at a glance with clean visualizations and summary statistics (totals, minima, maxima, and trends).

What you get: - Customizable metric cards - Trend indicators (7-day, 30-day, 90-day) - Summary statistics at a glance - Quick access to detailed views

Deep HealthKit Coverage

Activity, vitals, sleep, body composition, nutrition, and more—organized by category and searchable.

Categories covered: - Movement & Fitness - Heart & Circulation - Respiratory Health - Sleep & Recovery - Body Measurements - Nutrition & Hydration - Mindfulness & Mental Wellbeing - Environmental Factors

Scientific Context (Not Just Numbers)

For each metric, Health Analytics can show:

  • What it measures - Clear definitions in plain language
  • Why it matters clinically - Physiological and health relevance
  • How devices estimate it - Sensor technology and methods
  • Typical adult reference ranges - With scientific citations
  • Common confounders and limitations - What can affect accuracy

This context helps you interpret your data intelligently and know when to seek professional advice.

Flexible Export Engine

Export only what you need, for the time period you choose.

Supported formats: - CSV - For spreadsheets and data analysis - JSON - For developers and integrations - PDF - Professional reports for medical consultations - HTML - Web-friendly summaries - Markdown - Documentation and notes - Plain text - Universal compatibility

Export options: - Select specific metrics or categories - Choose custom date ranges - Include or exclude scientific context - Generate summary or detailed reports

Export History

Keep a record of what you exported and when. Useful for: - Tracking what you've shared with healthcare providers - Maintaining a personal audit trail - Quickly re-exporting similar datasets

Professional Reports

Generate readable PDF summaries suitable for medical consultations.

Report features: - Clean, professional formatting - Key metrics highlighted - Trend visualizations - Reference ranges included - Date and time stamped

Complete Privacy

No accounts, no cloud lock-in, no tracking. Health Analytics uses HealthKit permissions and keeps processing on-device.

Privacy guarantees: - No user accounts required - No data transmitted to servers - No analytics or tracking SDKs - No advertising - Full GDPR compliance - You control your data completely

Data Provenance

Know exactly where your data comes from: - Apple Watch measurements - iPhone sensor data - Third-party connected devices - Manual entries

This transparency reduces confusion and improves trust in your health data.
