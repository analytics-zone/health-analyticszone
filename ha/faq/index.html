Ta yaya zan fitar da bayanin lafiyar Apple Health zuwa CSV?

Tare da Health Analytics, fitar da bayanin lafiyar Apple Health ɗinka zuwa CSV abu ne mai sauƙi:

  1. Sauke Health Analytics daga App Store
  2. Buɗe aikace-aikacen kuma ba da izinin HealthKit
  3. Je zuwa auna da kake son fitar da ita
  4. Taɓa maɓallin Fitar da zaɓi tsarin "CSV"
  5. Zaɓi kewayon kwanan wata ɗinka da wuraren bayanai musamman
  6. Raba ko adana fayilin CSV

Fayilin CSV zai buɗe a Excel, Google Sheets, Numbers, ko kowane aikace-aikacen jadawali. Ba kamar fitarwar XML ta asali ta Apple ba, fayilolin CSV ɗin mu ana iya amfani da su nan take don nazari.

Zan iya raba bayanin HealthKit ɗina da likitana?

Ee! Health Analytics yana sauƙaƙa raba bayanin lafiyar ku tare da masu ba da kiwon lafiya. Kuna iya samar da rahotannin PDF na ƙwararru waɗanda suka haɗa:

  • Auna lafiya da aka zaɓa masu dacewa da alƙawarin ku
  • Taswirar diddigin da ke nuna canje-canje tsawon lokaci
  • Kewayon tunani na likita don mahallin
  • Bayanan tushen bayanai (Apple Watch, iPhone, da sauransu)

Kawai fitar da bayanai a matsayin PDF kuma ka aika wa likitan ku kafin alƙawarin ku, ko kawo kwafin da aka buga. Tsarin ya an tsara shi don bayyanawa da ƙwararanci don shawarwarin likita.

Menene bambanci tsakanin XML da CSV a fitar da bayanin lafiya?

Fitarwar bayanin lafiya ta asali ta Apple tana ba da fayilin XML, wanda ya cika amma yana da wahalar amfani da shi:

Fasali Fitarwa XML ta Apple Fitarwa CSV ta Health Analytics
Tsari Tsarin XML mai rikitarwa Tsarin jadawali mai sauƙi
Karatu Yana buƙatar ilimin fasaha Ana buɗewa kai tsaye a Excel/Sheets
Zaɓin Fitar Duka ko babu Zaɓi auna musamman

Tsarin CSV yana da kyau don nazarin bayanai, yayin da rahotannin PDF ɗin mu suna da cikakke don raba tare da masu ba da kiwon lafiya.

Shin bayanin lafiyar na yana cikin sirri lokacin amfani da Health Analytics?

Tabbas. Health Analytics an tsara shi ne tare da sanya sirri a matsayin fifikon farko:

  • Aiki 100% na gida — Duk nazarin bayanai yana faruwa a kan na'urarka
  • Ba a buƙatar asusun — Fara amfani nan take ba tare da rajista ba
  • Babu loda zuwa cloud — Bayanin lafiyar ku ba ya taɓa barin iPhone ɗin ku
  • Babu bin diddigin — Ba mu yi amfani da SDK ɗin nazari ko tattara bayanin amfani ba
  • Mai dacewa da GDPR — Cikakken bin ƙa'idodin sirri

Kuna sarrafa lokacin da kuma yadda ake fitar da bayananku. An adana fitarwa a gida a kan na'ura ɗin ku kuma ana raba su ne kawai lokacin da kuka zaɓi raba su.

Ta yaya zan canza Apple Health XML zuwa PDF?

Health Analytics yana canza bayanin HealthKit ɗin ku kai tsaye zuwa rahotannin PDF na ƙwararru:

  1. Buɗe Health Analytics a iPhone ɗin ku
  2. Zaɓi auna lafiya da kuke son haɗa su
  3. Zaɓi kewayon kwanan wata ɗin ku (mako na ƙarshe, wata, shekara, ko na musamman)
  4. Taɓa Fitar kuma zaɓi "Rahoto na PDF"
  5. Aikace-aikacen yana samar da PDF mai tsarawa tare da taswirar bayanai da mahallin
  6. Raba ta imel, AirDrop, ko adana zuwa Fayiloli

Ba sai ka fitar da XML da hannu kuma ka canza shi ba. Health Analytics yana karantawa kai tsaye daga HealthKit kuma yana ƙirƙirar PDF masu tsabta, masu karatu masu dacewa da alƙawuran likita.

Wane auna lafiya za zan iya fitar da shi daga HealthKit?

Health Analytics yana tallafawa fitar da duk manyan nau'ikan bayanai na HealthKit, an shirya ta rukunoni:

Motsi & Koshin Lafiya

Matakai, nisa, tashi sama, makamashi mai aiki, lokacin motsa jiki, VO₂ Max, motsa jiki

Zuciya & Jini

Bugun zuciya, bugun zuciya na hutawa, bambancin bugun zuciya (HRV), matsin jini, bayanin ECG, sanarwar gudu mara daidaito

Barci & Murmurewa

Tsawon barci, matakai na barci (REM, Core, Deep), lokaci a gadon, gurin numfashi

Auna Jiki

Nauyi, BMI, kashi % na kitse, ƙarfin tsoka, tsawo, kewayon ƙugu

Abinci Mai Gina Jiki & Ruwa

Shigar makamashi, macronutrients (carbs, furotin, kitse), micronutrients, amfani da ruwa

Labaran Likita & Lab

Glucose na jini, HbA1c, isar da insulin, abin da ke cikin barasa na jini, cike da iskar oxygen

Ana iya fitar da duk auna ɗaya ko a haɗa su, a cikin tsarin CSV, JSON, ko PDF.

Shin ina buƙatar Apple Watch don amfani da Health Analytics?

A'a, ba kwa buƙatar Apple Watch. Health Analytics yana aiki tare da kowane bayanai a cikin aikace-aikacen Apple Health ɗin ku, gami da:

  • Bayanin firikita na iPhone (matakai, tashi sama, nisa)
  • Auna Apple Watch (idan kuna da ɗaya)
  • Na'urorin da aka haɗa na ɓangare na uku (sikelin mai wayo, na'urorin matsin jini, mita glucose)
  • Rubuce-rubuce na hannu da ka ƙara zuwa aikace-aikacen Health
  • Bayanai daga wasu aikace-aikacen lafiya da koshin lafiya

Da yawa bayanai da kuka samu a Apple Health, da yawa fahimtar da za ku samu, amma ba a buƙatar Apple Watch.

Nawa ne farashi na Health Analytics?

Health Analytics yana ba da gwaji kyauta na kwana 7 don ku bincika duk fasali kafin ku yanke shawarar. Bayan gwajin:

  • Kallo da nazarin duk bayanin lafiyar ku
  • Fitar da ba tare da iyaka ba a cikin tsarin CSV, JSON, da PDF
  • Shiga zuwa mahallin kimiyya da kewayon tunani
  • Sabuntawa na yau da kullum tare da sabbin fasali da auna

Ba a buƙatar asusun, kuma za ku iya soke a kowane lokaci. Bayananku koyaushe suna kan na'urarka.

Zan iya amfani da Health Analytics don dalilai na bincike?

Ee! Health Analytics yana da kyau don binciken lafiya na sirri da bin diddigin kansa:

  • Fitar JSON — Tsarin bayanai mai tsari don rubutun nazari
  • Fitar CSV — Shigo da su a R, Python, SPSS, ko kayan ƙididdiga
  • Kewayon kwanan wata na musamman — Fitar da lokutan musamman don nazari
  • Asalin bayanai — Sanin wace na'ura ta rubuta kowace ma'auni
  • Nassoshi na kimiyya — Kewayon tunani da ke goyon bayan binciken da aka yi duba

Masu amfani da yawa suna fitar da bayanansu don binciken sirri, ayyukan ilimi, ko don raba tare da nazarin bincike.
