Bibiyar Zagayowar Haila

Ma'anarsa

Tarin nau'ikan bayanan lafiyar haihuwa a cikin HealthKit: -Ruwan Haila- Kwanaki na lokaci da ƙarfin kwarara -Sakamakon Gwajin Ovulation- Sakamakon gwajin gida -Ingancin ƙwayar mahaifa- Alamar haihuwa -Jinin Haila- Hange tsakanin lokuta -Ayyukan Jima'i- Bibiya na zaɓi

Me Yasa Yayi Muhimmanci

Bibiyar zagayowar yana ba da fa'idodin kiwon lafiya masu mahimmanci: -Zagaye na yau da kullun- Gano alamu na al'ada -Sanin haihuwa- Fahimtar tagogi masu haihuwa -Binciken lafiya- Zagaye na yau da kullun na iya nuna al'amura -Alamun daidaitawa- Alamun alamomi zuwa lokacin zagayowar -Shirye-shiryen likita- Bayanai don ziyarar kiwon lafiya

Yadda Ake Binsa

  • Shigar da hannu- Shigar da kwanakin lokaci, ƙarfin kwarara
  • Apple Cycle Tracking- Gina cikin Lafiya app
  • Yanayin wuyan hannu- Yana haɓaka tsinkaya (Series 8+)
  • Apps na ɓangare na uku- Yawancin aiki tare da HealthKit

Dabi'un Magana

Abubuwan Bibiya

Ruwan Haila

  • Haske, matsakaici, nauyi, tabo
  • Tsawon lokaci
  • Daidaitowar zagayowar

Ciwon mahaifa

Alamar haihuwa tana canzawa ta zagayowar: - Busasshiya/mai ɗaure → Ƙananan haihuwa - Creamy → Matsakaicin haihuwa - Farin kwai (mai mikewa, bayyananne) → Yawan haihuwa

Gwajin Ovulation

Home LH (luteinizing hormone) gwaje-gwaje: - Korau/Mai kyau/Mai girma - Surge yana nuna ovulation ~ 24-48 hours

Amfani da Cases

  • Hasashen lokaci- Sanin lokacin da ake tsammanin jinin haila
  • Shirye-shiryen haihuwa- Gano mafi yawan kwanakin haihuwa
  • Kula da lafiya- Bibiyar rashin bin ka'ida
  • Sanin PMS- Yi hasashen bayyanar cututtuka
  • Tarihin likita- Raba bayanan sake zagayowar tare da masu samarwa

Lokacin Tattaunawa da Likita

  • Kewaya gajarta fiye da 21 ko fiye da kwanaki 35
  • Lokacin bata (ba ciki ba)
  • Jini mai nauyi sosai
  • Ciwo mai tsanani
  • Jini tsakanin al'ada
  • Zubar da jini bayan menopause
  • Canje-canje kwatsam a cikin tsari

Bayanan sirri

Bayanan bin diddigin zagayowar yana da mahimmanci. Bibiyar Zagayowar Apple: - Adana bayanai akan na'urorin ku kawai - Ƙarshen-zuwa-ƙarshen rufaffen idan an kunna madadin iCloud - Karkashin ikon ku
