Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about exporting and analyzing your Apple Health data

How do I export my Apple Health data to CSV?

With Health Analytics, exporting your Apple Health data to CSV is simple:

  1. Download Health Analytics from the App Store
  2. Open the app and grant HealthKit permissions
  3. Navigate to the metric you want to export
  4. Tap the Export button and select "CSV" format
  5. Choose your date range and specific data points
  6. Share or save the CSV file

The CSV file will open in Excel, Google Sheets, Numbers, or any spreadsheet application. Unlike Apple's native XML export, our CSV files are immediately usable for analysis.

Can I share my HealthKit data with my doctor?

Yes! Health Analytics makes it easy to share your health data with healthcare providers. You can generate professional PDF reports that include:

  • Selected health metrics relevant to your appointment
  • Trend charts showing changes over time
  • Clinical reference ranges for context
  • Data source information (Apple Watch, iPhone, etc.)

Simply export the data as a PDF and email it to your doctor before your appointment, or bring a printed copy. The format is designed to be clear and professional for medical consultations.

What's the difference between XML and CSV health data export?

Apple's native health data export provides an XML file, which is comprehensive but difficult to use:

Feature Apple's XML Export Health Analytics CSV Export
Format Complex XML structure Simple spreadsheet format
Readability Requires technical knowledge Opens directly in Excel/Sheets
Selective Export All or nothing Choose specific metrics
Date Ranges Entire history only Custom date ranges
Medical Use Not doctor-friendly Professional PDF reports

CSV format is ideal for data analysis, while our PDF reports are perfect for sharing with healthcare providers.

Is my health data private when using Health Analytics?

Absolutely. Health Analytics is designed with privacy as the top priority:

  • 100% local processing — All data analysis happens on your device
  • No accounts required — Start using immediately without registration
  • No cloud uploads — Your health data never leaves your iPhone
  • No tracking — We don't use analytics SDKs or collect usage data
  • GDPR compliant — Full compliance with privacy regulations

You control when and how to export your data. Exports are saved locally to your device and shared only when you choose to share them.

How do I convert Apple Health XML to PDF?

Health Analytics automatically converts your HealthKit data into professional PDF reports:

  1. Open Health Analytics on your iPhone
  2. Select the health metrics you want to include
  3. Choose your date range (last week, month, year, or custom)
  4. Tap Export and select "PDF Report"
  5. The app generates a formatted PDF with charts and context
  6. Share via email, AirDrop, or save to Files

No need to manually export XML and convert it. Health Analytics reads directly from HealthKit and creates clean, readable PDFs suitable for medical appointments.

What health metrics can I export from HealthKit?

Health Analytics supports exporting all major HealthKit data types, organized by category:

Movement & Fitness

Steps, distance, flights climbed, active energy, exercise time, VO₂ Max, workouts

Heart & Circulation

Heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), blood pressure, ECG data, irregular rhythm notifications

Sleep & Recovery

Sleep duration, sleep stages (REM, Core, Deep), time in bed, respiratory rate

Body Measurements

Weight, BMI, body fat percentage, lean body mass, height, waist circumference

Nutrition & Hydration

Energy intake, macronutrients (carbs, protein, fat), micronutrients, water consumption

Clinical & Labs

Blood glucose, HbA1c, insulin delivery, blood alcohol content, oxygen saturation

All metrics can be exported individually or in combination, in CSV, JSON, or PDF format.

Do I need an Apple Watch to use Health Analytics?

No, you don't need an Apple Watch. Health Analytics works with any data in your Apple Health app, including:

  • iPhone sensor data (steps, flights, distance)
  • Apple Watch measurements (if you have one)
  • Third-party connected devices (smart scales, blood pressure monitors, glucose meters)
  • Manual entries you've added to Health app
  • Data from other health and fitness apps

The more data you have in Apple Health, the more insights you'll get, but an Apple Watch is not required.

Can I use Health Analytics for research purposes?

Yes! Health Analytics is ideal for personal health research and self-tracking:

Many users export their data for personal research, academic projects, or to share with research studies.
